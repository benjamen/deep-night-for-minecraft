# Store listing draft: Deep Night

**Status: draft.** Write the final listing only after the pack has been played in a world (see "Before you list").

**Title:** Deep Night
**One line:** Nights that get truly dark. Carry a light, and listen.

## Description
Minecraft's night is gloomy but never frightening. Deep Night lets it fall from dusk to a real, pitch-black midnight and
lift again before dawn. The only way to see is light: a torch, a lantern, a candle, a campfire, each making a circle of
light that grows with its brightness and wavers like a real flame.

- **A natural darkness:** follows the clock, deepens toward midnight, holds in the small hours, eases off before dawn. Moon phase and cloud change it.
- **Light matters:** the brighter the light, the farther you see; step away and your eyes hold the glow for a moment.
- **Night sounds:** crickets, owls, wind in the branches, a distant howl, a snapping twig, your own heartbeat in the dark.
- **Your choice of dark:** gentle, normal or pitch. Caves keep their own dark.
- **Optional:** a longer night, and no sleeping through the dark.

## Commands (cheats on)
`/scriptevent dn:mode gentle|normal|pitch` · `dn:long 1-4` · `dn:nosleep` / `dn:nosleep off` · `dn:time 19000` · `dn:auto` · `dn:status`

## Requirements
Minecraft Bedrock 1.26 or newer. Scripting API 2.10. Works best with the default day and night cycle.

## Before you list
- Play a full night at each mode; check torches, lanterns and candles each look right and the fog never flickers.
- Listen to the generated night sounds and decide if they pass; swap in recorded royalty-free sounds if not.
- Distant lights are hidden by the fog (a limit of Bedrock fog); say so plainly in the listing.
